Prayer Times in Alexandria, Egypt

One city, one sky: why Alexandria has its own timetable.

Alexandria sits at 31.20° N, 29.92° E, in the Eastern European Time (UTC+2) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Alexandria cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Egypt

Egypt follows the Egyptian General Authority of Survey convention: Fajr at 19.5 degrees and Isha at 17.5 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Alexandria. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Alexandria

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Alexandria changes by about 3.9 hours. On the Mediterranean coast, Alexandria's Fajr runs noticeably later than Cairo's in winter because of its higher latitude.
