Prayer Times in Taif, Saudi Arabia

One city, one sky: why Taif has its own timetable.

Taif sits at 21.27° N, 40.42° E, in the Arabia Standard Time (UTC+3) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Taif cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia follows the Umm al-Qura convention: Fajr at 18.5 degrees and Isha a fixed 90 minutes after Maghrib. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Taif. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Taif

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Taif changes by about 3 hours. Taif's mountain elevation, around 1,900 m, means the sun visibly sets a little later than at sea level — one reason city-level timetables matter.
