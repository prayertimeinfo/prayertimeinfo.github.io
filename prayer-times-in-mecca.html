Prayer Times in Mecca, Saudi Arabia

One city, one sky: why Mecca has its own timetable.

Mecca sits at 21.42° N, 39.82° E, in the Arabia Standard Time (UTC+3) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Mecca cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia follows the Umm al-Qura convention: Fajr at 18.5 degrees below the horizon and Isha a fixed 90 minutes after Maghrib (120 minutes in Ramadan). This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Mecca. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Mecca

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Mecca changes by about 3 hours. As the home of the Umm al-Qura calendar, Mecca is the reference city the whole method is built around.
