Prayer Times in Marrakesh, Morocco

One city, one sky: why Marrakesh has its own timetable.

Marrakesh sits at 31.63° N, 8.01° W, in the Western European Time (UTC+1, kept year-round) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Marrakesh cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Morocco

Morocco follows the Moroccan Ministry of Islamic Affairs convention: Fajr at 19 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Marrakesh. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Marrakesh

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Marrakesh changes by about 4 hours. Inland and further south than Casablanca, Marrakesh sees earlier sunrises and a slightly tighter day-length swing.
