Prayer Times in Johor Bahru, Malaysia

One city, one sky: why Johor Bahru has its own timetable.

Johor Bahru sits at 1.49° N, 103.74° E, in the Malaysia Time (UTC+8) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Johor Bahru cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Malaysia

Malaysia follows the JAKIM (Malaysian Islamic Development Department) convention: Fajr at 20 degrees and Isha at 18 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Johor Bahru. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Johor Bahru

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Johor Bahru changes by only about 10 minutes. Almost on the equator, Johor Bahru's sunrise and sunset are nearly constant — Maghrib sits around 7 pm all year.
