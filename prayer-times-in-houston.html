Prayer Times in Houston, United States

One city, one sky: why Houston has its own timetable.

Houston sits at 29.76° N, 95.37° W, in the Central Time (UTC-6, UTC-5 in summer) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Houston cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in United States

United States follows the ISNA (Islamic Society of North America) convention: Fajr and Isha both at 15 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Houston. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Houston

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Houston changes by about 3.6 hours. Houston's southern latitude keeps its timetable comparatively gentle — nothing like the swings of the northern states.
