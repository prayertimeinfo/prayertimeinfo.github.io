Prayer Times in Kano, Nigeria

One city, one sky: why Kano has its own timetable.

Kano sits at 12.00° N, 8.52° E, in the West Africa Time (UTC+1) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Kano cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Nigeria

Nigeria follows the Muslim World League (commonly used in Nigeria) convention: Fajr at 18 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Kano. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Kano

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Kano changes by about 1.2 hours. In northern Nigeria, Kano's timetable is steady through the year — the variation here is minutes, not hours.
