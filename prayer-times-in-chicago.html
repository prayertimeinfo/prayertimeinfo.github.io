Prayer Times in Chicago, United States

One city, one sky: why Chicago has its own timetable.

Chicago sits at 41.88° N, 87.63° W, in the Central Time (UTC-6, UTC-5 in summer) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Chicago cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in United States

United States follows the ISNA (Islamic Society of North America) convention: Fajr and Isha both at 15 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Chicago. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Chicago

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Chicago changes by about 6.1 hours. At nearly 42 degrees north, Chicago's summer Isha falls late and its winter Fajr later still — the seasonal swing is real.
