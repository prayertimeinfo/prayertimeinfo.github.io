Prayer Times in Marseille, France

One city, one sky: why Marseille has its own timetable.

Marseille sits at 43.30° N, 5.37° E, in the Central European Time (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Marseille cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in France

France follows the no single national standard convention: French mosques commonly follow the UOIF 12-degree method or the Muslim World League method, so local mosque timetables can differ by several minutes. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Marseille. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Marseille

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Marseille changes by about 5.9 hours. France changes its clocks twice a year, so Marseille's timetable jumps by an hour overnight in March and October on top of the solar drift.
