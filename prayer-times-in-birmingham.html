Prayer Times in Birmingham, United Kingdom

One city, one sky: why Birmingham has its own timetable.

Birmingham sits at 52.48° N, 1.90° W, in the GMT (UTC+0, UTC+1 in summer) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Birmingham cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in United Kingdom

United Kingdom follows the no single national standard convention: UK mosques publish their own timetables, commonly built on twilight angles between 15 and 18 degrees, so two mosques in one city can differ. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Birmingham. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Birmingham

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Birmingham changes by about 9 hours. Above 52 degrees north, Birmingham's summer days stretch past 16 hours — fasting Ramadan in June is a genuinely northern experience.
