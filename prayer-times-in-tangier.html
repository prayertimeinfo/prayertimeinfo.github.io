Prayer Times in Tangier, Morocco

One city, one sky: why Tangier has its own timetable.

Tangier sits at 35.76° N, 5.83° W, in the Western European Time (UTC+1, kept year-round) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Tangier cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Morocco

Morocco follows the Moroccan Ministry of Islamic Affairs convention: Fajr at 19 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Tangier. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Tangier

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Tangier changes by about 4.6 hours. Morocco's northernmost major city, Tangier has the country's longest summer days and latest summer sunsets.
