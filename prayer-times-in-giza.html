Prayer Times in Giza, Egypt

One city, one sky: why Giza has its own timetable.

Giza sits at 30.01° N, 31.21° E, in the Eastern European Time (UTC+2) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Giza cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Egypt

Egypt follows the Egyptian General Authority of Survey convention: Fajr at 19.5 degrees and Isha at 17.5 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Giza. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Giza

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Giza changes by about 3.7 hours. Giza shares a skyline with Cairo but its own coordinates — a reminder that even neighbouring cities get their own timetable.
