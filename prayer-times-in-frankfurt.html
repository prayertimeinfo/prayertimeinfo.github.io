Prayer Times in Frankfurt, Germany

One city, one sky: why Frankfurt has its own timetable.

Frankfurt sits at 50.11° N, 8.68° E, in the Central European Time (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Frankfurt cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Germany

Germany follows the Diyanet (widely used in Germany) convention: many German mosques follow the Diyanet calculation with Fajr at 18 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Frankfurt. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Frankfurt

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Frankfurt changes by about 8.6 hours. At 50 degrees north, Frankfurt's summer fasts are among the longest in Germany.
