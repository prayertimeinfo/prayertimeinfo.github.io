Prayer Times in Medina, Saudi Arabia

One city, one sky: why Medina has its own timetable.

Medina sits at 24.47° N, 39.61° E, in the Arabia Standard Time (UTC+3) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Medina cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia follows the Umm al-Qura convention: Fajr at 18.5 degrees and Isha a fixed 90 minutes after Maghrib. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Medina. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Medina

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Medina changes by about 3.2 hours. Medina sits roughly 340 km north of Mecca, so its dawn and sunset run a few minutes apart from Mecca's on the same day.
