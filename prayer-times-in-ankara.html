Prayer Times in Ankara, Turkey

One city, one sky: why Ankara has its own timetable.

Ankara sits at 39.93° N, 32.86° E, in the Turkey Time (UTC+3) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Ankara cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Turkey

Turkey follows the Diyanet (Turkish Presidency of Religious Affairs) convention: Fajr at 18 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Ankara. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Ankara

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Ankara changes by about 5.5 hours. Turkey keeps UTC+3 year-round, so Ankara's timetable moves only with the sun, not with clock changes.
