Prayer Times in Mumbai, India

One city, one sky: why Mumbai has its own timetable.

Mumbai sits at 19.08° N, 72.88° E, in the India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Mumbai cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in India

India follows the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi (commonly used in India) convention: Fajr and Isha both at 18 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Mumbai. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Mumbai

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Mumbai changes by about 2.2 hours. India also uses a single time zone; coastal Mumbai's sea horizon makes its sunset Maghrib clean and punctual.
