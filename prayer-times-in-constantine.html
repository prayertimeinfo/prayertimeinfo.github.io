Prayer Times in Constantine, Algeria

One city, one sky: why Constantine has its own timetable.

Constantine sits at 36.37° N, 6.61° E, in the Central European Time (UTC+1) zone. Prayer times follow the sun, not the clock, so Constantine cannot simply borrow a timetable from the next city over — a few hundred kilometres of longitude moves every prayer by minutes, and latitude reshapes the whole year.

The method used in Algeria

Algeria follows the Algerian Ministry of Religious Affairs convention: Fajr at 18 degrees and Isha at 17 degrees below the horizon. This is the single biggest reason prayer apps disagree — two apps set to different methods can show Fajr fifteen minutes apart for the same street in Constantine. When you check a timetable, check the method first.

Seasons in Constantine

Between the longest and shortest day of the year, daylight in Constantine changes by about 4.5 hours. In Algeria's east, Constantine's prayers run roughly half an hour ahead of Oran's on the same day.
